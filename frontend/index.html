Framework Architecture

Pi-Index filters noise and yields quantitative results strictly aligned with Responsible Research Assessment (RRA) and CoARA (Coalition for Advancing Research Assessment) guidelines.

Intake & Identity Extraction & Heuristics Multi-LLM Panel Pidyne Judge Ledger & Chain Outputs

End-to-end processing flow: intake and identity, extraction and deterministic scoring, the independent model panel, Pidyne adjudication, and Proof-of-Research settlement on Sepolia.

Scoring Pipeline in Detail

Stage Reference

StageComponentWhat Happens
1Unified Intake & ZK-Identity RegistryORCID iD / W3C DID verification, local PDF, Unpaywall DOI resolution and OpenAlex discovery, ZK double-blind assignment.
2Extraction & Deterministic ScoringPyMuPDF layout-aware text extraction, MDAR adherence, RRID validation, reproducibility markers, empirical density.
3Independent Model PanelLlama, Mistral, Qwen and Gemini each assess the manuscript separately, alongside the local Scilem structural analyser.
4Pidyne AdjudicationCross-model evidence synthesis, inter-model agreement measurement, judgement-quality grading, adversarial logic integrity matrix.
5Proof-of-Research & TokenomicsBlock validation, zk-SNARK proof generation, criteria-weight recording, soulbound piQ minting on Sepolia.
6Dossiers, Cartography & ForecastingCoARA/DORA dossiers, AI defence rebuttal strategy, global map of science, Pidyne LSTM epoch forecasting.

CoARA Compliance & Core Pillars

  • Diverse Research Outputs (C5 & C8): structurally evaluates open datasets, code repositories, and containerized executable environments.
  • Qualitative & Quantitative Balance (C1–C8): algorithms act as auditors, not replacements for peer review.
  • Transparency & Researcher Sovereignty: evaluation weights, logic states, and criteria scores are hashed and stored on Ethereum (Sepolia). Researchers retain sovereign ownership via DID/ORCID.
