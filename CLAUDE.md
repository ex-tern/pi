# Working on ScholarPi with Claude

ScholarPi runs as two copies of this repository:

| Site | Branch | Railway environment | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| https://pitechlab.com | `main` | production | Stable. Real users, real data. |
| https://exp.pitechlab.com | `experimental` | experimental | Evolves constantly. May break. Separate data volume. |
| https://se.pitechlab.com (also super.pitechlab.com) | `super` | its own environment | The superellipse site (`frontend/superellipse.css`, on by host name `se.*` or `super.*`, `SCHOLARPI_SHAPE=superellipse` or `?shape=se`). Deploys alone from `super`, branched from `experimental` on 2026-10-10. Changes the owner asks for on se. go to `super`. |

## Rules for Claude

1. **Work on `experimental`.** Branch from it, commit to it, and push to it when the owner asks for changes. That deploys to exp.pitechlab.com automatically.
2. **Never push to `main`, and never merge into it, without the owner explicitly saying so in that conversation.** Promoting means merging `experimental` into `main` after the owner has looked at the experimental site.
3. **Before every push, run:**
   - `cd backend && python -m pytest -q`: compare against the known baseline (12 failures that predate the Lab). Do not add new failures. (`tests/test_forecast.py` runs again since 2026-10-10: it now imports `pid_engine`, the forecasting module's current name.)
   - `python scripts/build_hal.py --src ../HAL-OS`: refuses to build if `frontend/hal/wire.js` and HAL-OS disagree.
   - Load the site in a browser at desktop and 390 px width: no page errors, no horizontal scroll.
4. **The repository is public.** Never commit secrets, `.env` files, or anything from private projects (those live on the server volume; see `backend/lab.py`).
5. **Keep the design language** in `frontend/theme.css`:
   - ink on paper, with one cobalt accent for "here / interactive"
   - status colours muted
   - Geist type, sentence case
   - the π circle as the only ornament

   Change it deliberately, not by accident.
6. **The base image comes from `public.ecr.aws`, not Docker Hub.** Railway builds hit Docker Hub's pull limit ("429 Too Many Requests" at "load metadata for docker.io/library/python"), which fails the build in seconds. Keep the `FROM` on the ECR mirror.
7. **`app.js` is large and load-bearing.** Prefer small, separate modules (`lab.js`, `orbit.js` and `channel.js` are the pattern) over growing it.

## The superellipse look (frontend/super/): the rule book

The front end is a **SuperAI program**: `super/pitechlab.super.js` (plain JSON-compatible data) says what exists and how it is wired; `super/runtime.js` loads it as `SuperAI.program` (or the visitor's edited copy, `superai:program`, when it passes `SuperAI.validate`), and `super/superai-rs.js` runs the language's own engine, written in Rust (`rust/superai`, built to `super/superai.wasm` by `scripts/build_superai.py`; `node frontend/super/superai.test.mjs` tests it). The program's parts: `sources` (native blocks: parts of index.html whose cards become windows), `titles`, `hide`, `merges`, `bubbles` (what each bubble holds, and `at`, where it starts), `diagram` (the supers and wires a first visit starts with: `supers` with kind, text, hint, size, at, everywhere, desktop; `wires` as "super.port" pairs, where "site" is the inner super, "main" the main super, "title" the title super and "bubble:Title" a bubble), `live` (the Live window's source, refresh and the functions that write its lines), `functions` (small parts of the site written in SuperAI and run by the Rust engine: percent, ago, liveStatus, liveCounts, add; `SuperAI.call(name, inputs)`) and `project` (the project map). Change the site's structure in the program, not in the modules; the windows' insides stay native.

**Drawn by three.js** (owner, 2026-10-12: "use an open source graphical engine"; super/engine.js, three.js r185 vendored in frontend/vendor/three, MIT): one WebGL canvas under the page draws the light behind the glass, the main and inner supers, every super's frosted pane (from its superellipse distance field: fill, ink edge, iridescent sheen when hovered or selected, soft shadow), every wire (strips sampled from the wires' own svg paths, dots flowing on hover), the lattice and every port sparkle; the page's elements keep their size, place and behaviour and stop painting their chrome (html.se-gl, super/glass.css). A software renderer (no graphics processor) or frames slower than 28 ms fall back to the CSS glass look; `?gl=1` forces the engine (tests here run without a GPU, so use it to see the engine).

**Everything is a super**, drawn the same way (super/super.css, the uniform block at its end; the material in super/glass.css): frosted glass over a deep warm light drifting slowly behind the page, amber, honey and sage with a soft vignette, embers and a fine grain (owner, 2026-10-12: "add this glass effect", "improve the glass ui"); each pane has a bright bevel along its top, a faint prism split at its edge and a wide warm shadow; every rounded corner on the page, inside windows too, is a superellipse corner (owner: "make everything superellipses, not round corners"; super/super.css :where(*) rule, and the drawings' boxes are superellipse paths), with an iridescent sheen running round the edge of the hovered or selected super, a 1.5 px ink edge, a superellipse at n = π (Shift + scroll gives one its own n, kept as se:n:<name>), four port sparkles on its sides (input left, output right, sync top, condition bottom; super/site.js places them and reads their values), a lock at its lower right (super/lock.js: a scroll in on it locks, out unlocks; locked, what is inside stays inside and it goes into no other super; se:lock:<name>), and the same hover (a hint of cobalt on its edge) and selection (a click, a cobalt edge; super/select.js: the supers nest and one is selected at a time). The supers: the **main super** (the loop round everything, super/loop.js: its ports a little inside the display's edge), the **inner super** (the site, super/site.js: locked in place and as big as the main super lets, its edge just inside the main super's side ports and just clear of the title super, super/loop.js siteMargins; no zoom, no drag), the **title super** ("pi tech lab" on one line, top-left corner, super/logo.js; a scroll on it resets the view once the view has changed; the 0 key too), the **bubbles** (Lab, Library, Tools, Explore, Connect; expandable, super/bubble.js: compact, a square of side --se-compact, the largest of which ten fit in the inner super (super/loop.js compactSide), with a small drawing of its name wired to its windows; every scroll step grows it a little toward its open size, its drawing giving way to its name and pills, and back (super/bubble.js zoom, --sb-t); open, it is its drawing grown up (owner, 2026-10-12): its name in a box on the left wired by soft curves (super/bubble.js links) to one labelled box per window in a column on the right, words never under 12 px; a scroll out on a pill shrinks the bubble; a wire dropped on it opens it; a scroll in on one of its pills expands that pill into its window; se:bubble:<key>), the **windows** (open at one uniform width in the middle of the inner super and no taller than their content (desktop; super/zoombox.js fitHeight), no bar: the content's own title shows and the window moves by the blank margin inside its edge; content fitted whole and zoomed, never scrolled, super/zoombox.js), the **project super** (the whole project as a SuperAI program, super/project.js: compact it is the same species as a bubble, drawn by the same function (SeBubble.drawing: its name's box, "Project", wired to a box per section); every scroll step grows it a little, the bubble drawing fading as the map comes in (--pj-map), until it fills the inner super, and scrolled on past that it shows the running program to edit; it drags and follows its ports as a bubble does), and the **Super AI supers** (super/nodes.js: the central one, id site-box, says "run"; a double click on bare space makes one that says "prompt" (windows open or not); type a question and Enter asks every engine, but only when it is connected to run by wires or sits inside it (connectedToRun), else it shows its question and says to connect it first; numbers and operators compute locally (2, 3 → + is 5; 3, 5 with + attached is 8; a word typed into a super is its label, not a question, unless it ends with "?": "math" with 4 and 3 wired in and + attached at any of its sparkles shows 7, and "show result" wired from it shows 7 too; a Super AI super takes what is wired to any of its sparkles as an input, a wire from another super's sync or condition sparkle carrying that super's result, data flowing from an output or else the way the wire was drawn; the site's and the main super's ports are the lattice, not data; an attached operator super is its operation; rust/superai lang.rs engine() agrees); a ■ scrolled past 150 px is a holder that holds supers (see-through; inside it is like the page; it moves by its label strip or edge); its n follows its size and use (autoN) until Shift + scroll gives it one; Shift + scroll on a Super AI super or a holder changes only its shape).

**The start** (a first visit, or after the app is reset): the site at zoom 1, both supers near-boxes (n = 24), five compact bubbles laid out left to right (their `at`), the central super alone, and the wires main.sync → site.sync → run.sync, title → site.sync and site.condition → main.condition; nothing else (lv:nodes, lv:start-wired, lv:intro-gone).

**Nothing is clickable, only zoomable** (owner, 2026-10-12), except wires and the prompts typed into Super AI supers: a click on a super selects it and lets you type into it, and presses nothing (stop, pause and ask go by wires and Enter); the project map's supers are not buttons; the lock locks by a scroll in on it and unlocks by a scroll out (the keyboard still toggles it); the title super resets the view by a scroll on it once the view has changed; the project super shows the program by a scroll on past the whole map (a scroll out on its head, or Escape, closes it; ⌘/Ctrl + Enter runs it, ⌘/Ctrl + S downloads it, ⌘/Ctrl + Shift + Backspace goes back to the shipped program); the windows' insides stay native (forms, links, buttons).

**A scroll** (super/scroll.js, one rule): on bare space it does nothing (the inner super is locked); on a super it sizes the super (Alt + scroll zooms its text); every super scrolls to expand (owner, 2026-10-12): on a bubble or the project super every step grows or shrinks it a little; on a pill a few steps in open its window; in a window it zooms the content with every step and grows the window while there is room (back out: content first, then window; Alt + scroll zooms the content alone); Alt + scroll on the title or the palette sizes it; **only Shift + scroll changes an n** (the ring is not a control). One gesture stays on what it started on for 400 ms; a gesture that ends within 6 % of 100 % settles on 100 %; Safari's pinch works the same; the view glides (super/motion.js).

**Wiring** (super/wire.js): a wire passes behind the supers it does not join (clipped out of them); a wire into a sync or condition port arrives vertically, never running along an edge; a port shows no extra star where a wire meets it, and ports under a window that is not theirs are hidden; port names (the site's and the main super's too) show only while a wire is being drawn. Then: from an edge or a port at once (the pointer is a + there), from a word only after holding the press still for 280 ms (or with Alt), so a drag or a selection never starts a wire; inside a holder the same (a click on a holder's bare space over a wire reaches the wire); a wire snaps to a port within 26 px; a wire's two ends that together name a window open it (SuperLink.intent); a new wire touching a pill, a window, a compact bubble or Live opens it; **deleting a wire cancels what it did** (windows close, the bubble compacts, a Super AI super cut off from run stops and shows its question again); a double click deletes a wire, a click selects it (cobalt) for Delete, for text wires, bubble wires and node wires alike (one helper: SuperLink.selectable); hovering on a wire shows data flowing along it; wires are graphite, 1.5 px; **removing the title's wire into the site's sync resets the app** (everything kept under lv:, orbit:, se: and superai: is forgotten and the page reloads).

**Too small, a super closes** (owner, 2026-10-12): scrolled on out at its smallest, a window closes (its content all in view first) and a Super AI super or holder fades away with its wires (a holder with what it holds; never the central super); an open bubble or the project super compacts, which is its closing.

**Superellipses in every browser** (owner, 2026-10-12: "make everything superellipses, not round corners"): every rounded corner in the look is `corner-shape: superellipse(…)` (super/super.css); browsers without corner-shape (Safari, Firefox) get super/squircle.js, which paints each rounded element's background and border as an SVG superellipse of its size and colours (its own border-radius cut to 0.6 so the shape is drawn whole; no backdrop blur or box shadow outside it); `?sq=1` turns it on anywhere for testing.

**Everything always fully readable** (owner, 2026-10-12; super/legible.js, super/zoombox.js): type set under 11 px in a window's content is lifted to 11 px; a window's content is never fitted smaller than the scale that keeps its smallest type at 11 px on screen (the readable floor): a window too small for that grows (as far as the inner super lets, super/loop.js winCap), and content still bigger than its window moves by dragging it (the pointer is a hand); a window you sized yourself keeps its size. Anything else drawn under 11 px on screen becomes a super, a small paper box the size of its words (the same kind as the boxes in a compact bubble's drawing); zoom in and it is text again. This covers every text in the look (super/legible.js ROOTS: windows' content, the project map, bubbles and their drawings, Super AI supers and holders, the project super, the title), so the zoomed-out start shows boxes, never unreadable type.

**No sudden movement** (owner, 2026-10-12): everything moves slowly and steadily; super/motion.js stretches every tween (SLOW); bubbles' layout, bubbles growing step by step, windows pushed aside or sized to fit, a window's content zooming, a super stepping off another, the project super stepping off a window and text turning into boxes all glide or fade; the page has no zoom feedback (the inner super is locked).

**Nothing overlaps unless it is inside a super**: bubbles step out of the way of windows, supers and the project super (orbit.js obstacles), windows push each other apart and step off supers, gliding (supers never jump: the project super glides aside too) (super/loop.js separateWindows, keepWindowsIn: a window stays inside the inner super and, unless you sized it, takes no more than about half of it), a super dropped on anything steps off it (clear), the central super never steps aside. **Dropped outside the inner super, a thing is gone on purpose**: a super is deleted with its wires (a holder with what it holds; the central super comes back to the middle), a bubble is gone until the app is reset (orbit:gone:<key>), a window closes.

**Phones** (narrower than 700 px): supers marked `desktop` are left out, a window may take most of the inner super and never shrinks below a readable size, compact bubbles are smaller (compactSide), a swipe never opens a window, and a finger on an edge must hold before it wires.

**Live** is a window in the Explore bubble (live.js adds the pill; PiLive.open/close open and close it); its lines are written in SuperAI (`live`, `liveStatus`, `liveCounts`, `ago`). The side panel and its tab exist only in the round look.
