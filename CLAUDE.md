# Working on ScholarPi with Claude

ScholarPi runs as two copies of this repository:

| Site | Branch | Railway environment | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| https://pitechlab.com | `main` | production | Stable. Real users, real data. |
| https://exp.pitechlab.com | `experimental` | experimental | Evolves constantly. May break. Separate data volume. |
| https://se.pitechlab.com (also super.pitechlab.com) | `super` | its own environment | The superellipse site (`frontend/superellipse.css`, on by host name `se.*` or `super.*`, `SCHOLARPI_SHAPE=superellipse` or `?shape=se`). Deploys alone from `super`, branched from `experimental` on 2026-10-10. Changes the owner asks for on se. go to `super`. |

## Rules for Claude

1. **Work on `experimental`.** Branch from it, commit to it, and push to it when the owner asks for changes. That deploys to exp.pitechlab.com automatically.
2. **Never push to `main`, and never merge into it, without the owner explicitly saying so in that conversation.** Promoting means merging `experimental` into `main` after the owner has looked at the experimental site.
3. **Before every push, run:**
   - `cd backend && python -m pytest -q`: compare against the known baseline (12 failures that predate the Lab). Do not add new failures. (`tests/test_forecast.py` runs again since 2026-10-10: it now imports `pid_engine`, the forecasting module's current name.)
   - `python scripts/build_hal.py --src ../HAL-OS`: refuses to build if `frontend/hal/wire.js` and HAL-OS disagree.
   - Load the site in a browser at desktop and 390 px width: no page errors, no horizontal scroll.
4. **The repository is public.** Never commit secrets, `.env` files, or anything from private projects (those live on the server volume; see `backend/lab.py`).
5. **Keep the design language** in `frontend/theme.css`:
   - ink on paper, with one cobalt accent for "here / interactive"
   - status colours muted
   - Geist type, sentence case
   - the π circle as the only ornament

   Change it deliberately, not by accident.
6. **The base image comes from `public.ecr.aws`, not Docker Hub.** Railway builds hit Docker Hub's pull limit ("429 Too Many Requests" at "load metadata for docker.io/library/python"), which fails the build in seconds. Keep the `FROM` on the ECR mirror.
7. **`app.js` is large and load-bearing.** Prefer small, separate modules (`lab.js`, `orbit.js` and `channel.js` are the pattern) over growing it.

## The superellipse look (frontend/super/): the rule book

The front end is a **SuperAI program**: `super/pitechlab.super.js` (plain JSON-compatible data) says what exists and how it is wired; `super/runtime.js` loads it as `SuperAI.program` (or the visitor's edited copy, `superai:program`, when it passes `SuperAI.validate`), and `super/superai-rs.js` runs the language's own engine, written in Rust (`rust/superai`, built to `super/superai.wasm` by `scripts/build_superai.py`; `node frontend/super/superai.test.mjs` tests it). The program's parts: `sources` (native blocks: parts of index.html whose cards become windows), `titles`, `hide`, `merges`, `bubbles` (what each bubble holds, and `at`, where it starts), `diagram` (the supers and wires a first visit starts with: `supers` with kind, text, hint, size, at, everywhere, desktop; `wires` as "super.port" pairs, where "site" is the inner super, "main" the main super, "title" the title super and "bubble:Title" a bubble), `live` (the Live window's source, refresh and the functions that write its lines), `functions` (small parts of the site written in SuperAI and run by the Rust engine: percent, ago, liveStatus, liveCounts, add; `SuperAI.call(name, inputs)`) and `project` (the project map). Change the site's structure in the program, not in the modules; the windows' insides stay native.

**Drawn by three.js** (owner, 2026-10-12: "use an open source graphical engine"; super/engine.js, three.js r185 vendored in frontend/vendor/three, MIT): one WebGL canvas under the page draws the light behind the glass, the main and inner supers, every super's frosted pane (from its superellipse distance field: fill, ink edge, iridescent sheen when hovered or selected, soft shadow), every wire (strips sampled from the wires' own svg paths, dots flowing on hover), the lattice and every port sparkle; the page's elements keep their size, place and behaviour and stop painting their chrome (html.se-gl, super/glass.css). A software renderer (no graphics processor) or frames slower than 28 ms fall back to the CSS glass look; `?gl=1` forces the engine (tests here run without a GPU, so use it to see the engine).

**Everything is a super**, drawn the same way (super/super.css, the uniform block at its end; the material in super/glass.css): frosted glass over a deep warm light drifting slowly behind the page, amber, honey and sage with a soft vignette, embers and a fine grain (owner, 2026-10-12: "add this glass effect", "improve the glass ui"); each pane has a bright bevel along its top, a faint prism split at its edge and a wide warm shadow; every rounded corner on the page, inside windows too, is a superellipse corner (owner: "make everything superellipses, not round corners"; super/super.css :where(*) rule, and the drawings' boxes are superellipse paths), with an iridescent sheen running round the edge of the hovered or selected super, a 1.5 px ink edge, a superellipse at n = π (Shift + scroll gives one its own n, kept as se:n:<name>), four port sparkles on its sides (input left, output right, sync top, condition bottom; super/site.js places them and reads their values), a lock at its lower right (super/lock.js: a scroll in on it locks, out unlocks; locked, what is inside stays inside and it goes into no other super; se:lock:<name>), and the same hover (a hint of cobalt on its edge) and selection (a click, a cobalt edge; super/select.js: the supers nest and one is selected at a time). The supers: the **main super** (the loop round everything, super/loop.js: its ports a little inside the display's edge), the **inner super** (the site, super/site.js: locked in place and as big as the main super lets, its edge just inside the main super's side ports and just clear of the title super, super/loop.js siteMargins; no zoom, no drag), the **title super** ("pi tech lab" on one line, top-left corner, super/logo.js; a scroll on it resets the view once the view has changed; the 0 key too), the **bubbles** (Lab, Library, Tools, Explore, Connect; expandable, super/bubble.js: compact, a square of side --se-compact, the largest of which ten fit in the inner super (super/loop.js compactSide), with a small drawing of its name wired to its windows; every scroll step grows it a little toward its open size, its drawing giving way to its name and pills, and back (super/bubble.js zoom, --sb-t); open, it is its drawing grown up (owner, 2026-10-12): its name in a box on the left wired by soft curves (super/bubble.js links) to one labelled box per window in a column on the right, words never under 12 px; a scroll out on a pill shrinks the bubble; a wire dropped on it opens it; a scroll in on one of its pills expands that pill into its window; se:bubble:<key>), the **windows** (open at one uniform width in the middle of the inner super and no taller than their content (desktop; **A scroll** (super/scroll.js, one rule): on bare space it does nothing (the inner super is locked); on a super it sizes the super (Alt + scroll zooms its text); every super scrolls to expand (owner, 2026-10-12): on a bubble or the project super every step grows or shrinks it a little; on a pill a few steps in open its window; in a window it zooms the content with every step and grows the window while there is room (back out: content first, then window; Alt + scroll zooms the content alone); Alt + scroll on the title or the palette sizes it; **only Shift + scroll changes an n** (the ring is not a control). One gesture stays on what it started on for 400 ms; a gesture that ends within 6 % of 100 % settles on 100 %; Safari's pinch works the same; the view glides (super/motion.js).

**Wiring** (super/wire.js): a wire passes behind the supers it does not join (clipped out of them); a wire into a sync or condition port arrives vertically, never running along an edge; a port shows no extra star where a wire meets it, and ports under a window that is not theirs are hidden; port names (the site's and the main super's too) show only while a wire is being drawn. **Superellipses in every browser** (owner, 2026-10-12: "make everything superellipses, not round corners"): every rounded corner in the look is `corner-shape: superellipse(…)` (super/super.css); browsers without corner-shape (Safari, Firefox) get super/squircle.js, which paints each rounded element's background and border as an SVG superellipse of its size and colours (its own border-radius cut to 0.6 so the shape is drawn whole; no backdrop blur or box shadow outside it); `?sq=1` turns it on anywhere for testing.

**Everything always fully readable** (owner, 2026-10-12; super/legible.js, super/zoombox.js): type set under 11 px in a window's content is lifted to 11 px; a window's content is never fitted smaller than the scale that keeps its smallest type at 11 px on screen (the readable floor): a window too small for that grows (as far as the inner super lets, super/loop.js winCap), and content still bigger than its window moves by dragging it (the pointer is a hand); a window you sized yourself keeps its size.
